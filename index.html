How to play
  • Players take turns flicking the ball directly.
  • Drag back from the ball to set direction and power, then release.
  • Pegs are defined on the left side and mirrored exactly across midfield.
  • Goals, field lines, and elastic band rails are modeled in 3D. First to 5 wins.
