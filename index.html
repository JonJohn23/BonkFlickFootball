How to play
  • Each player has 3 pucks and takes turns flicking one puck.
  • Drag from one of your pucks to set direction and power.
  • Peg positions are arranged to mimic a Binho-style board layout.
  • Goals, field lines, and elastic band rails are modeled in 3D. First to 5 wins.
